# Zenya AI — Weekly Industry Report
**Week of July 6, 2026**

---

## 1. AI Website Builders

**Wix** shipped an Automations Assistant (April) letting users build triggers from natural language, and added voice support to Aria (February). Wix Harmony remains the most feature-complete AI builder — generates full sites in ~60 seconds with on-canvas AI editing. **Squarespace** still has no AI site generator; its AI is limited to text rewriting and brand-voice copy. **Framer** is consolidating its lead as the design-quality pick for marketing sites and portfolios. **Durable** continues its 30-second site pitch but hasn't announced new features. The market is splitting into three lanes: design-first (Framer, Webflow), all-in-one business (Wix, Hostinger, Durable), and code-first full-stack (Bolt.new, Lovable, v0) — the last category is evolving fastest, adding database integrations and moving toward full-stack.

## 2. No-Code / Low-Code Market

Expert consensus forecasts $7-8B in total no-code funding for 2026. By 2026, 70% of new business apps use LCNC tech. **Blaze** acquired No Code No Problem (podcast/TikTok channel) — content-as-distribution play. Hot investment sectors: AI-driven code generation, embedded enterprise no-code, and Web3 no-code platforms. The overall market is projected to hit $101.7B by 2030. No major new entrants this week.

## 3. Shopify Ecosystem

**Summer '26 Editions** dropped June 17 with 150+ updates. Headline: **Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)** now enabled by default on every store — lets AI shopping agents read catalogs and build carts automatically. New "Agentic" admin section tracks agent-driven sales. Other key updates: **Horizon theme system** with 10 new free themes (Fabric, Ritual, Vessel), native A/B testing for themes/checkout, AI Collection Sort and Predictive Cross-Sell blocks built into admin, Checkout Components at GA replacing Scripts. **Scripts officially died June 30** — any store still running them broke on July 1. Dawn theme performance: LCP down 18%, CLS halved. OS 2.0 adoption now at 78% of active stores.

## 4. Arabic & MENA Market

Saudi e-commerce projected at **$25B in 2026** — largest and fastest-growing in the GCC. 75%+ of regional retailers now using AI per SAMENA Council. **Arabic.AI** (UAE) launched Pronoia, an agentic AI platform purpose-built for Arabic dialects. **Nanovate** (Egypt) raised $1M for Arabic-first AI automation supporting 22 dialects. **Shahbandr** launched an Arabic AI toolkit specifically for e-commerce. **Actualize** is building Arabic conversational AI for GCC dialects, integrated into CNTXT AI's voice platform Munsit. The Arabic AI tooling ecosystem is rapidly maturing — multiple startups now competing on dialect coverage and vertical specialization.

## 5. One Opportunity

**Ship UCP/agentic commerce support for Zenya's Shopify-exported themes.** Shopify just made UCP default on every store, meaning AI shopping agents can now discover and sell products automatically. Zenya's store and collective templates that export as Shopify themes should explicitly support UCP metadata and the new Horizon theme system. Marketing angle: "Your Zenya store is AI-agent-ready from day one — customers find you even when they shop through ChatGPT." This positions Zenya ahead of competitors who haven't updated their Shopify theme exports for the agentic commerce shift.
