# Zenya AI - Company Profile & System Context

## 1. Mission Statement
**To democratize e-commerce design by allowing anyone, regardless of technical skill or budget, to launch a professional, high-converting Shopify store in seconds using Artificial Intelligence.**

## 2. What is Zenya AI?
Zenya AI is a cutting-edge **AI-powered Shopify Theme Generator**. 
Unlike traditional theme marketplaces where users buy a static template and spend days customizing it, Zenya AI builds a **custom-tailored theme** for the user on the fly.

We are not just a theme; we are the **engine** that builds themes.

## 3. Core Value Proposition
*   **Speed**: Go from "idea" to "ready-to-sell store" in under 60 seconds.
*   **Zero Skill Required**: No coding, no design experience, and no copywriting needed.
*   **Conversion-First**: Every generated theme is built on a high-performance, mobile-optimized "Master Template" designed for sales.
*   **Cost-Effective**: Eliminates the need for expensive agencies or premium themes ($350+).

## 4. How It Works (The User Journey)
1.  **Input**: The user provides a simple text prompt (e.g., *"A minimalist skincare brand for sensitive skin"*) OR a product link (e.g., AliExpress URL).
2.  **AI Processing**: 
    *   **Copywriting**: The AI writes compelling headlines, product descriptions, and "About Us" text.
    *   **Branding**: It selects a color palette (primary/secondary colors) and typography that matches the brand vibe.
    *   **Layout Strategy**: It intelligently selects and orders sections (Hero, Features, Testimonials) based on the niche.
3.  **Generation**: The system injects this content into our robust **Zenya Master Theme** (Liquid/JSON).
4.  **Delivery**: The user downloads a complete `theme.zip` file, ready to upload to Shopify.

## 5. The "Zenya Master Theme" (Technical Foundation)
All generated themes are based on our proprietary **Zenya Master Theme**, which is:
*   **Shopify 2.0 Compatible**: Full support for "Sections Everywhere" and app blocks.
*   **Performance Optimized**: Alpine.js and Tailwind CSS for lightning-fast load times.
*   **Clean & Error-Free**: Rigorously tested to ensure no 404s, valid schemas, and strict Shopify compliance.
*   **Modular**: Contains 40+ conversion-focused sections (Countdown Timer, Sticky Add-to-Cart, Volume Bundles, Comparison Tables) that the AI can mix and match.

## 6. Target Audience
*   **Dropshippers**: Need to test products fast without spending days building stores.
*   **New Entrepreneurs**: Have a product idea but get stuck on the "tech" side of building a website.
*   **Agencies & Freelancers**: Use Zenya to generate base client stores instantly, then charge for customization.
*   **Small Business Owners**: Brick-and-mortar stores moving online.

## 7. Key Features & Capabilities
*   **Text-to-Store**: Generate a full store from a single sentence.
*   **Link-to-Store**: (Future) Scrape a product URL to auto-generate a branded store for that specific item.
*   **Smart Copywriting**: AI writes sales copy that focuses on benefits, not just features.
*   **Visual Identity**: Automatic color and font selection based on color psychology.
*   **Conversion Elements**: Built-in scarcity (timers), social proof (reviews), and trust badges.
