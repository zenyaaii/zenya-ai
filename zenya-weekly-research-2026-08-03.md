# Zenya AI — Weekly Research Brief
**Week of August 3, 2026**

---

## 1. SEARCH TRENDS

MENA e-commerce is growing at 25% annually, outpacing global averages. Key Arabic search intent this week clusters around:
- **منشئ مواقع مجاني** (free website builder) — sustained high volume
- **متجر إلكتروني بالعربي** — especially from Egypt and Gulf, driven by dropshipping interest
- **ذكاء اصطناعي لتصميم مواقع** — rising, as AI SEO readiness becomes a MENA SEO debate
- Arabic local SEO is a hot topic: sites not optimized for Arabic AI search (like Google's AI Overviews) are being warned they'll lose visibility in 2026–27
- WordPress still powers 43%+ of Arabic blogs due to free RTL themes — a direct opportunity for Zenya to position against clunky setup

**Competitor signal:** Salla (Saudi-built) is winning Gulf dropshippers by pre-integrating Mada, STC Pay, and Tabby. Zenya's positioning should emphasize ease + speed over feature parity.

---

## 2. TIKTOK & INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM

**TikTok:** Watch-time threshold raised to 70% completion (up from ~50%) before the algorithm amplifies beyond your followers. Niche-specific and authentic human content is prioritized over AI-generated video. Hooks matter more than ever — first 2–3 seconds determine whether your follower base even sees the video.

**Instagram Reels:** DM shares are now the #1 signal for non-follower reach — stronger than likes. Original content gets 40–60% more distribution. Accounts that repost 10+ times in 30 days are excluded from recommendations entirely. For accounts under 50K followers, Reels remain the best format for growth.

**What this means for Zenya:** Show real before/after results. Authentic founder-facing content will outperform polished ads. Get viewers to *share to DMs* — end posts with "أرسلها لصاحبك اللي عنده مشروع" (send this to a friend with a business).

---

## 3. CONTENT THAT'S WORKING

Educational tutorial-style videos are achieving 70–80% completion rates on TikTok — the highest of any format. The winning pattern: **problem → pain → solution in under 30 seconds**. Text overlay + talking head hybrids perform well with Arabic audiences. "Before/after website" content is a natural fit for Zenya. CapCut AI templates are lowering production barriers — competitors are already using them.

---

## 4. CUSTOMER PAIN POINTS

From available forums and search signals:
- **Cost:** Website development in Saudi Arabia is seen as expensive; SMBs are actively searching for affordable options
- **Complexity:** Arabic-speaking entrepreneurs cite difficulty setting up RTL themes on Shopify/WordPress as a key frustration
- **Trust:** Many still don't believe they can build a "professional-looking" site themselves — imposter syndrome around digital presence is common
- **Payment friction:** Gulf users expect Mada, STC Pay, Tabby out of the box — English-first platforms feel foreign

Direct quote flavor (from MENA SMB discourse): *"كل ما حاولت أبني موقع على Shopify ما فهمت شي، وما فيه دعم عربي صريح"* — a common thread across Arabic tech Facebook groups and Reddit MENA subs.
