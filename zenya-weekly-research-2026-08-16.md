## 1. SEARCH TRENDS

Searches around **منشئ مواقع بالذكاء الاصطناعي** (AI website builder) and **إنشاء متجر إلكتروني** (create online store) are highly active across the Arabic web. Al Jazeera Net published a roundup of top website platforms (March 2026) — indicating mainstream Arabic media is now covering website builders, signaling growing mass-market awareness. Key Arabic search phrases driving traffic:

- منشئ مواقع مجاني (free website builder)
- إنشاء موقع بالذكاء الاصطناعي مجاناً
- متجر إلكتروني بالذكاء الاصطناعي
- تصميم مواقع بدون برمجة

Competitors appearing in Arabic SERPs: Appy Pie (Arabic localized), CodeDesign.ai, Kleap, DeepSite AI, Wix. **Zenya's gap to exploit**: none of these are purpose-built for the MENA small business owner — they're global tools with Arabic language bolted on.

---

## 2. TIKTOK & INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM — August 2026

**TikTok**: Algorithm now heavily weights **watch time over view count**. Favoring niche-specific content and **authentic human creators over AI-generated video**. Sweet spot length: **60–180 seconds**. First-hour engagement is critical for push. New test: "Don't Show on Profile" — videos that reach the feed without cluttering the grid (useful for sales content).

**Instagram**: **Shares via DMs** are now the #1 ranking signal — more important than likes. Reels with **clear voiceover + keyword-rich speech** rank higher in Instagram Search. Algorithm rewards content that keeps users on the platform across multiple formats (Reels → Stories → carousels).

**What this means for Zenya**: Post Reels with a strong hook that drive DM shares ("أرسل لصاحبك اللي ما عنده موقع"). Use voiceover with keywords like "موقع إلكتروني" and "متجر" so Instagram Search surfaces the content organically.

## 3. CONTENT THAT'S WORKING

Arab TikTok creators continue to grow fast in 2026. What's performing:

- **Before/after transformations**: "قبل وبعد" format — show an ugly/unprofessional page → then a sleek AI-built result. High rewatch value, high share rate.
- **"I built this in X minutes" hooks**: Speed + AI = shareability. "بنيت موقعي كامل في 3 دقائق بالذكاء الاصطناعي" drives curiosity clicks.
- **Pain point call-outs with text overlay**: Pointing at a poorly designed competitor site or generic Linktree and saying "هذا اللي يخليك تخسر عملاء."

Format working best: **talking head with text overlay + screen recording cut** (show the AI build process in fast-forward).

## 4. CUSTOMER PAIN POINTS

Real complaints from Shopify users (community forums + Capterra reviews, 2026):

- **"Expensive"** — the single most-logged complaint (156 instances on Capterra). Apps, templates, and transaction fees stack up fast.
- **Steep learning curve** — users report needing HTML/CSS knowledge just to move elements. "I canceled my first attempt and still can't figure it out."
- **Support decline** — offshore teams, inconsistent answers, no escalation.
- **Not built for Arabic-speaking sellers** — payment localization and RTL design are afterthoughts.

**Direct opportunity**: Arabic-speaking small business owners feel priced out and overwhelmed by Shopify. They want something simple, affordable, and built for them.
