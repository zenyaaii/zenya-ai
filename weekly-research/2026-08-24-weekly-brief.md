## 1. SEARCH TRENDS

## 2. TIKTOK & INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM — August 2026

**Instagram:** DM shares are now the #1 ranking signal for Reels (outweighing likes by a wide margin). Saves rank #3. Original content gets 40–60% more distribution; accounts with 10+ reposts/month are excluded from recommendations entirely. For small business accounts: carousels optimised for saves, Reels designed to prompt DM-shares ("أرسل هذا لصاحبك الي عنده متجر").

**TikTok:** Algorithm weights watch-completion curves and re-watch rates over follower counts. TikTok is testing a "don't show on profile" post type — useful for A/B testing hooks without cluttering your grid.

**Key takeaway:** Both platforms are rewarding *original, locally-made content*. Being an Arabic-first business is an edge right now.

## 3. CONTENT WORKING RIGHT NOW

AI-powered Arabic text-to-speech tools (e.g. NourVoice) are going viral in creator circles — hook format: tool demo with voiceover in Egyptian/Gulf dialect. The "AI script for short videos" niche is blowing up: accounts showing ChatGPT/Gemini prompts that write TikTok hooks are racking up shares. Format: screen-record + talking head overlay. Duration: 30–45 seconds.

## 4. CUSTOMER PAIN POINTS

Facebook group post (real quote, 3iada group): *"عملت موقع على Shopify لكن غالي"* — user frustrated by cost. Shopify's Arabic localization is repeatedly cited as painful: foreign templates don't support RTL properly without paid themes ($100–$300). Community forums flag the design complexity: any serious customisation requires Liquid knowledge or hiring a developer. These are Zenya's clearest conversion arguments.
