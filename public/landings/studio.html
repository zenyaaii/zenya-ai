A letter from the founder

We make things
slowly.

For nine years we've built a small studio of nine people who believe the best work happens away from urgency. Here's how we got here, and what we make.

2017

The first project

A neighbourhood café asked us to redesign their menu. We took six weeks. They're still our oldest client.

2021

The atelier

We moved into a converted ironworks on the river. Quiet floors, soft light, real plants.

2026

Where we are now

Nine of us, ninety projects, one principle: the small studio that says no a lot, so it can say yes properly.

What we believe

Three quiet rules.

01.

Make less, better.

Volume is the enemy of craft. We take five clients a year.

02.

Stay small.

Nine people. Everyone touches every project. No hierarchy.

03.

Take time.

If it's worth doing, it's worth a season.
